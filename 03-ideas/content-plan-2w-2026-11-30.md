# EARND: 2-week content plan

6 posts, 3 a week (Mon / Wed / Fri), rotating the four pillars from
`CONTENT_BRIEF.md`: **the magic · the method · the EARNED moment · the problem**.
Read the brief first: it has the approved and banned claims, brand colours,
fonts and voice.

**Platforms:** TikTok + Instagram Reels (same vertical video, 1080×1920).
Use the X variant for text posts where given.

**Timing**
- Week 1 (Mon 30 Nov – Fri 4 Dec): **pre-launch**. The app is in App Store
  review. CTA = "Coming to iPhone soon. Follow for launch day."
- Week 2 (Mon 7 – Fri 11 Dec): **launch week**, *if Apple has approved and
  the app is released*. CTA = "EARND is on the App Store. Link in bio."
  If not yet live, keep the pre-launch CTA and don't say "out now".

**Rules for every post**
- One lime (`#C6FF3D`) highlight per frame, on the number that matters.
  Off-white (`#F5F4F0`) text on near-black (`#0B0C0D`). Inter Bold.
- On-screen text: big, max 6 words per card, safe zone away from the
  bottom 20% and right edge (platform UI).
- The demo clips use **sample data** ("Alex", "90 → 102.5 kg"). Never say
  "my progress" or "I gained". Say "this is what it looks like".
- Never: "free", "free trial", "AI", guaranteed results, Android, Paul
  Carter's name or face.
- Sound: trending, low-key instrumental or gym ambience. No voiceover
  needed; text carries it. Add captions if you do add voice.
