- **Working sets:** 2 per exercise · **Rep range:** 4–8 · **Rest:** 2–3 min between working sets · **Warm-ups:** as needed, not counted
- **Progression:** Reps → Load → Repeat. Priority: Execution → Reps → Load.

## Progression rules (founder's wording)
Target: 4–8 clean reps.
1. Pick a load you can perform for at least 4 clean reps.
2. Keep the load the same and add reps over time.
3. Once you own 8 clean reps with full ROM and execution, increase the load.
4. The new load should bring you back toward 4–5 reps.
5. Build the new load toward 8 and repeat.
6. If adding weight compromises ROM or execution, you haven't earned the increase.

Example: 100 kg × 5 → × 6 → × 7 → × 8 → increase load → 105 kg × 4–5 → build toward 8 → increase again.
