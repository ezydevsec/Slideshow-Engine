## Persona 1: The inconsistent tracker

Already lifts, but uses Notes or memory and doesn't know what to aim for next session.

- **Situation:** 18–35, training regularly, beginner to intermediate. Logs in the Notes app or not at all. Knows the exercises but picks weights by feel. *Assumption:* trains 3–4 times a week and has done so for a few months or more.
- **Main frustration:** can't remember what they lifted last time, so every session starts with guessing the weight and reps. Can't tell from their notes whether they're improving. (Brief pains 1, 2, 3, 8.)
- **Buying trigger:** seeing last session's numbers next to today's target. The thought: "I don't have to remember my last workout or guess what to do today." *Assumption:* the trigger lands mid-scroll, close to a gym session.
- **Objection:** "why pay when notes or a free app does the same thing?" (Brief objection 1.) Answer by showing the convenience in one screen, not a feature list.
- **EARND screen:** the magic-moment exercise screen. Machine Chest Press, "Last session: 100 kg × 7, 100 kg × 6", "Today's target: 100 kg × 8 on your first set", weight pre-filled, rest timer starts on completion.
- **Voice (assumption):** lowercase, short, gym slang ("PR", "push day", "progressive overload"), few emojis.
- **Warm-up search terms (assumption):** progressive overload, gym tracker, how to track workouts, gym notes, upper lower split.
- **Account theme (assumption):** gym-progress and lifting-tips page.

**Provisional hooks (copy hypotheses):**
- H1-01 "i can't remember what i lifted last time" *(brief pain 1, test first)*
- H1-02 "i know the exercises. i just never know what weight to aim for" *(brief pain 2, test first)*
- H1-03 "my notes app is full of numbers and i still can't tell if i'm improving" *(brief pain 8)*
- H1-04 "do i add weight or try for more reps? i never know" *(brief pain 3)*

## Persona 2: The busy returning lifter

Wants a ready-made programme and needs training days to fit a changing schedule.

- **Situation:** 18–35, coming back after a break or fitting the gym around shifts, study, or a changing work week. Doesn't want to design a programme. *Assumption:* trains 2–4 times a week, depending on the week.
- **Main frustration:** every app wants them to build their own routine, and fixed splits fall apart as soon as a day is missed. Spends more time deciding what to train than training. (Brief pains 5, 6, 7, 9, 10.)
- **Buying trigger:** a simple four-day Upper/Lower plan they just follow, plus moving any of the four sessions to the day they're free. *Assumption:* the trigger is the "just give me one plan" relief more than scheduling detail.
- **Objection:** "what if i can't train four days every week?" (Brief objection 3.) Show that the four prescribed sessions can be moved to available days. The programme does not adapt to fewer sessions, so never imply it does. Secondary: "can i try it before paying?" (no free trial, brief objection 2).
- **EARND screen:** the programme summary with the four-day Upper/Lower structure and chosen training days, and the reschedule flow. **No screenshot of either exists yet.** Until one does, this persona's sets use the magic-moment screen.
- **Voice (assumption):** lowercase, tired-but-motivated, references to shifts, uni, and "getting back into it".
- **Warm-up search terms (assumption):** getting back in the gym, beginner workout plan, 4 day upper lower, gym routine for busy people, workout plan for shift workers.
- **Account theme (assumption):** simple-routines and getting-back-into-it page.

**Provisional hooks (copy hypotheses):**
- H2-01 "every workout app wants me to build my own routine. just give me one" *(brief pain 9, test first)*
- H2-02 "i'm back in the gym and need a simple plan to follow" *(brief pain 10)*
- H2-03 "missed a workout. do i skip it or move everything?" *(brief pain 7)*
- H2-04 "i need a workout plan that fits my shifts" *(brief pain 6)*
