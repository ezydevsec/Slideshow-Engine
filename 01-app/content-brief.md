## 1. The app in one line

**EARND tells you exactly what to lift on every set, logs it in one tap, even
from your Lock Screen, and only adds weight when you've earned it.**

- Platform: **iPhone only**, iOS 18+. No Android, no web, no Apple Watch.
- Status: **pre-launch**, submitting to the App Store. Don't say "out now"
  until it's live; use "coming soon" / "join the waitlist".
- Price: **$29.99/year or $6.99/month** (local prices vary). Hard paywall
  after onboarding. **No free trial, no lifetime plan, no free version.**

## 2. Who it's for

- Lifters (mostly 18–35) who **train hard but stopped growing**: same weights
  for months, random workouts, no log.
- People who log in the **Notes app** or not at all.
- Evidence-based, natural lifters who like low-volume, heavy, Upper/Lower
  training and hate junk volume.
- Gym-goers who want to be told what to do, not build a programme.

## 3. The one problem we solve

Most people repeat the same weights for months because nobody tells them what
to beat today. Muscle grows when you get stronger in a good rep range: add a
rep, earn the weight, repeat. EARND does that thinking for you.

## 4. The training method (what the app actually does)

- **Upper / Lower**, 4 days a week: Upper A, Lower A, rest, Upper B, Lower B,
  rest, rest. (2–3 days a week also works; the sequence just rolls on.)
- **Two hard working sets** per exercise, each muscle trained twice a week.
  About 5–6 hard sets per muscle per week. No junk volume.
- **Double progression on 4–8 reps for every exercise.** Get every set to 8
  with clean form, then the weight goes up and you start back near 4–5.
- Sets taken close to failure (about 1 rep in reserve), not to failure.
- **Rest until recovered:** at least 3 minutes after big compound lifts,
  2:30 for single-joint work, 45–60 seconds between warm-ups.
- **Calves first** on lower days: long stretch at the bottom (3–5 s), up to neutral.
- Machine-based exercise list: chest press, wide-grip pulldown, chest-supported
  row, machine shoulder press, preacher curl, Poliquin pushdown, hip thrust,
  pendulum squat, leg extension, seated leg curl, adductor, calf press, RDL,
  pec deck, Bayesian curl and more. Approved swaps if a machine is taken.
- **Week 0** practice week to find your starting weights.

- "Know exactly what to lift on every set."

- "Weight only goes up when you've earned it."

## 7. Do NOT say

- Any guaranteed result ("add 10 kg in 4 weeks", "build X lbs of muscle").
  Targets are projections; label them that way.
- "Free", "free trial", "free version" or "lifetime deal". None exist.
- "AI" or "AI coach". There is no AI in the app.
- Android, Apple Watch, Apple Health, iCloud sync, nutrition or calorie tracking,
  cardio tracking, video exercise demos. Not in the app.
- Fake ratings, reviews, user counts or testimonials. Only real ones, with
  permission.
- **Paul Carter / @liftrunbang1**: the method follows evidence-based
  principles he also teaches, but **never use his name, face, clips or quotes,
  or imply he endorses EARND**, unless he agrees in writing.
- Medical or injury claims ("fixes back pain", "injury-proof").

- **Name:** EARND (always capitals). Tagline options: "Earn every kilo." /
  "Beat last week. Every week." / "Numbers that move."
- **Voice:** direct, confident, a bit blunt, gym-floor plain English. Short
  sentences. Numbers over adjectives. No hype words ("insane", "game-changer"),
  no fitness-influencer fluff.
- **Colours:**

  - Text `#F5F4F0` (off-white), secondary `#8A8E92`, tertiary `#5C6165`
  - Accent **lime `#C6FF3D`**: one accent per frame, for the number that matters
  - Cards `#1C1E20`, strokes `#2A2D30`

  highlight. No gradients, no stock gym photos, no mascot.
