> 1/ My first mobile app hasn't been submitted to the App Store yet.
> 
> So this week I collected other builders' rejections and payment problems instead, and turned them into a pre-submission checklist.
> 
> Five things on it so far:

> 2/ Attach the subscriptions to the build you submit.
> 
> One builder waited three weeks in review, then got rejected because the in-app purchases weren't attached to the submission.

> 3/ Turn on billing grace period in App Store Connect.
> 
> Another found that "not cancelled" didn't mean "paid": every trial ended in a failed payment. Grace period keeps access open while Apple retries the card.

> 4/ Test a real purchase and a restore on a second device.
> 
> If there's no login, restore is the only way someone gets back what they paid for on a new phone.

> 5/ Keep one way to reach your users that Apple doesn't control.
> 
> Account appeals rarely succeed. Builders without accounts are now adding an opt-in email for exactly this.
> 
> What would you add before I submit?
