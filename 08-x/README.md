# X (@ezyxbt) content workflow

Daily X briefs for Ezy's founder account. Separate from the TikTok/Instagram slideshow engine: different platform, different rules (see "Naming" below).

## Files

- `exclusions.txt`: every status ID ever recommended. Check it before every brief and append the new targets after it.
- `builders-list.txt`: app builders @ezyxbt follows. Preferred pool for reply targets and the 17:30 live sweep.
- `briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`: one brief per publication day (Europe/London).
- `metrics.csv`: weekly numbers Ezy pastes in (profile visits, follows, author replies). Leave unknown values blank.

## Naming (overrides the TikTok rule in CLAUDE.md)

On X, never name the app. Refer to it only as "my first mobile app", "the app", or by the user problem. Don't reveal private research, identifying mechanics, unreleased screens or sensitive commercial details (no prices on X).

## The brief

Deliver for the publication day:

1. Two original posts, both directly about mobile apps.
   - One proof-led build-in-public post, based on a real artefact, action, decision, mistake, constraint, lesson or result.
   - One broader mobile-app lesson, opinion or teardown that's useful to strangers.
   - Format by day. Mon, Tue, Thu and Fri: two short posts. Wed and Sun: one short post plus a 150–300-word post or a 4–6-post thread. Sat: one short post plus a genuine weekly review.
   - Visuals: at least one original a day carries a safe visual, such as a text card, a simple diagram, a checklist screenshot, an analytics screenshot, or a desk or setup photo. Never use unreleased app screens or anything that names the app.
   - Short posts and each thread post stay under 280 characters. Count them before delivering.
   - Give each one a publishing time, character count, format and safe media.
2. Twenty reply targets, ranked by freshness, plus an angle bank for the live sweeps.
3. The execution plan below.

Drafts only. Never publish or send anything.

## Reply-target rules

- A personal account of a mobile-app builder, indie founder or app analyst. Never companies, products, platforms, publications or analytics accounts.
- The post is directly about mobile-app building, product, onboarding, paywalls, subscriptions, retention, ASO, App Store operations, launch, distribution, monetisation or a concrete consumer-app case study.
- Published within 7 days. Prefer under 24 hours. Over 48 hours old at execution means backup only.
- Prefer accounts in `builders-list.txt`. Twenty different accounts per brief. Include at least three larger accounts (20k+ followers) for reach, alongside peers who are more likely to reply.
- Give the exact status URL and the verified publication date. Never invent a post, URL, date, quote or figure.
- Label external revenue as creator-reported, company-reported, estimated or unverified.
- Check `exclusions.txt` first. If fewer than twenty targets are valid, list only those and state the shortfall.
- Each target lists: account, link, date, context, why it fits, timing (NOW, WITHIN 1 HOUR or TODAY), and a 1–3-sentence reply. A reply adds an observation, question, supported disagreement or relevant experience. It never paraphrases the post. Write the reply text plainly, with no quotation marks around it, so it can be copied straight into X.

## Daily execution plan (about 55 minutes, London time, about 30 replies)

| Time | Block | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Morning (commute or break) | 5 replies from the brief (targets 11–15) | 8 |
| 13:00 | 5 replies from the brief (freshest targets 1–5), then publish Original 1 | 10 |
| 13:10–13:20 | Answer every genuine response, and reply to anyone who answered Ezy's replies | 5 |
| 15:30 (if free) | 5 replies from the brief (targets 16–20) or a mini live sweep | 8 |
| 17:30 | Live sweep: 5–10 replies on Builders-list posts under 60 minutes old, using the angle bank, plus targets 6–10 | 15 |
| 18:00 | Publish Original 2 | 2 |
| 18:00–18:20 | Conversation window: answer every reply, and follow up with any author who replied today | 8 |

Reply safety: about 30 to 40 replies a day, written by hand, and never more than about 15 in one sitting. No copy-paste or template replies, no links in replies, and no more than one reply per author per day unless they reply back. Ramp up over 2 weeks: about 20 a day in week 2, then 30 to 40. Stop and slow down if impressions on replies suddenly collapse.

Suggest a quote post only when the added point genuinely needs more space.

## Voice

Direct, calm, ambitious, slightly ruthless British English. Avoid: generic motivation, guru language, engagement bait, forced controversy, hashtags, emojis, fake urgency, AI-sounding phrasing, universal claims and over-explaining. Only ask a question when it's specific enough for experienced app builders to answer.

## Integrity

- Never claim planned work as done. An earlier AI draft is not evidence of progress.
- Never invent analytics, users, revenue, downloads or tests.
- Trading is inactive until Ezy says otherwise. Never imply Ezy is trading.
- With no new verified product progress, use a decision-led, research-led or learning-led angle.
