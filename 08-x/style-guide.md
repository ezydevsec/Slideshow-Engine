# X style guide (from @jakecastilloooo and @ErnestoSOFTWARE, 8–22 Nov 2026)

## What they do

- Volume: Jake posts about 2 originals a day; Ernesto about 1. Both post far more replies than originals (Jake 47, Ernesto 71 over two weeks), and most of those replies answer people commenting on their own posts.
- Reply length: the median is about 35–40 characters. "manual", "yes", "2 a day does the job". Fast and short, aimed at keeping the thread busy.
- Formatting: short lines, one idea per line, lots of white space. Lists with no bullets or with dashes. Mostly lowercase (Jake).
- Hook types that won:
  - A rule: "NEVER hire a head of growth before $50k/mo"
  - A list of proof: "take note: Calm, Express VPN, Manus are running AI animation ads"
  - A repetition shout: "HARD PAYWALL > FREEMIUM" repeated 14 times
  - Then and now: "2 years ago I had $9.50 in my bank account"
  - Contrarian: "I cannot stand MRR larpers"
  - A public experiment with promised updates: "30 tiktok accounts… posting updates for the next 30 days"
  - A free resource: "I open sourced a claude skill… link in comments". This was Ernesto's best post, with 6.4k bookmarks.
- Media: Ernesto attaches media to most posts (10 of 14).
- Credibility comes from revenue (Cal AI $50M ARR, $170k profit). That's the part Ezy can't borrow yet.

## How Ezy uses it

Copy:
1. Short-line formatting, mostly lowercase, one idea per line.
2. Run a public experiment as a series: the 30-day reply experiment, with numbers every Sunday.
3. One free, saveable resource a week, built from real material (checklists, decision docs, templates). Post the resource as an image, and put any link in a self-reply rather than in the main post.
4. Rules and lists, but only ones Ezy can back with something real.
5. Answer every commenter on Ezy's own posts within the first hour, in 1–8 words when that's enough.
6. Attach a visual to most originals.

Don't copy:
- Invented or borrowed revenue as personal credibility. Ezy's credibility is honest small numbers and real decisions.
- Hype words ("holy sh*t", "goldmine", "print money", "change your life"), emojis and guru claims.
- Claims that something "proves" a thesis after 3 days.

## Two reply modes

- Cold replies to other builders' posts: 1–2 short lines, lowercase, with the sharpest point first. Ask a question only when it's genuinely useful.
- Replies to comments on Ezy's own posts: as short as the answer allows, sent fast, and to every commenter.
