## Voiceover script v2 (about 205 words, about 95–100 s)
Use the **same voice as ep 1 v1** in Seed Audio, with the same Voice details. Paste exactly:

> If your arms look decent from the side... but the second you face the mirror, they look small... then this is what you need to know. Your arm grows in three directions. Width. Height. And depth. Most people only ever train one of them. If your arms look fine from the side but flat from the front, you've been ignoring width. And honestly, that makes sense, because almost nobody talks about the muscle behind it. The brachialis. It sits underneath your biceps, and pushes out the side of your arm. To train it, we use hammer curls. Same as a normal curl, with one change. Your grip. Instead of your palms facing up, turn them to face each other. That shifts the work off your biceps, and onto the brachialis. Curl the weight all the way up, and squeeze. Then lower it slowly, and feel the stretch. And when I say slowly, I mean it. Don't just drop the weight and let gravity do the work for you. Example: two sets of four to eight reps, as close to failure as you can. When you own eight clean reps, add weight. But here's the catch. You can't beat last week if you don't know what you did. EARND shows your last session, and the exact target to beat, on every set. Next up: height. EARND launches soon. Follow for the launch.
