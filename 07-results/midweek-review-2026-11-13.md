## Findings

A same-day step change across all formats points to account-level distribution, not one bad post. Its "good" level (~650) lasted only 8–10 Nov; before that it sat at ~250–300 for two weeks.
2. **All three accounts are posted within the same minute** (e.g. 13 Nov 01:55:04 / 01:55:16 / 01:55:46; 12 Nov 22:34–22:35; 20:32–20:33; 12:15–12:16). Three accounts from one phone posting simultaneously, four times a day, looks like a linked network to TikTok. It also means posts go out at 01:55 and 12:15 UK time instead of the planned UK evening slots.
3. **Nothing escapes the first test batch.** Every post on every account lands in the same 600–800 band (or ~230 on @ezyxbt now) and stops; likes are 1–3% of views and saves 0–1%. TikTok shows each post to its trial audience and doesn't push it further.
4. Earnie's two newest posts (comic rival video 496, a 91 still young) are too fresh to judge; his anatomy posts keep the best saves (11 on hamstrings/legs).
