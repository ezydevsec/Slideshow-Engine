## Script shape (escalator, transcribed)
"Imagine a man standing at the bottom of a giant escalator. At the top is the life he dreams about… But the escalator is moving down. He starts walking… after a few weeks he gets tired and stops to rest. Within days, the escalator pulls him all the way back down. Other people step on and just stand there… So he starts again, and this time he never stops. Some days he runs, some days he only takes one small step. One year later he reaches the top. Because in life you are never standing still. If you are not moving forward, you are moving back."

1. "Imagine a man…" + one visual metaphor (escalator, ladder, wall, bag).
2. A problem that shows the rule.
3. He fails once (stops, slides back).
4. He changes one thing and keeps going ("some days only one small step").
5. Two-line moral on screen.

## Visual
