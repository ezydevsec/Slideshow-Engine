## Slide by slide
1. **Hook.** Real founder gym photo + a short take lifters will argue with. 3 to 8 words.
2. **Why.** One line backing the take up. Opinion or plain fact, never an unconfirmed personal story.
3. **Tension.** What that costs you in the gym (tracking, targets, guessing). No physique claims.
4. **Release + CTA.** What EARND does, in one line, then "i'm building EARND. follow @earndapp for the launch."

## Where the app goes
Final slide only, as text. The @ezyxbt template (`06-queue/accounts.json`) says EARND appears on the final slide only and images are founder photos only, so no screenshot.

**Variant not yet allowed:** Stronger shows the app on slide 2 or 3 as the release. Testing that on @ezyxbt needs (a) the founder to change the @ezyxbt template and (b) a real photo of the founder logging a set in EARND (a listed gap in `01-app/founder-photo-catalog.md`).

## Hook templates (10, written for EARND)
1. unpopular opinion: your notes app isn't a training log
2. stop guessing your weights
3. most people don't know what they lifted last week
4. your notes app is lying to you
5. writing your lifts down isn't enough
6. "just add weight" isn't a plan
7. you don't need a new programme
8. the set you're about to do needs a target
9. hot take: switching programmes won't fix it
10. nobody checks their old workout notes

Founder-voice rule: a hook is an opinion or a question. Anything phrased as the founder's own past ("i used to...", "i stopped...") needs his confirmation first.

## Notes
- Square 1080×1080, real founder photos only (crop/resize, crop out other gym members), white text with black stroke, no slide-count labels.
- Never pair EARND copy with physique-forward shots (ezy-03, ezy-14).
- Reuse a hook that wins, with new photos.
