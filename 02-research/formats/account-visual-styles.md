# Visual style per account (proposal 2026-11-11, founder link: @tonistelk "10 animation styles to go viral", IG 875K plays)

The reference reel shows 10 styles (pixel art, chalkboard, glow sketch, PS2, comic, flat vector, minimal line, CGI motion, a brick-toy style, explainer 3D). Each of our accounts gets **one style it never shares**, so a viewer can tell which account they're watching in the first frame. Samples: `02-research/style-frames/`.

| Account | Style | Why | Production |
|---|---|---|---|
| @earndapp (brand) | **Graphite + lime brand look**: the graphite mascot, dark theme, real app screens | it's the product account; app screens and the mascot anatomy are its identity | as now |
| @earnie.lifts | **Comic art**: bold ink outlines, flat colour, halftone, panels and speech bubbles (sample `earnie-comic.png`) | comics are stories by nature (the founder's rule for Earnie), and it stops Earnie looking like @earndapp's mascot slides | ~1 Higgsfield image per comic page; panels and bubbles laid out in code |
| Line-art account (new) | **Glow sketch**: warm gold line drawings under a spotlight on black (sample `lineart-glow-sketch.jpg`) | the style of the founder's 6 line-art references; gold instead of @earndapp's lime keeps it separate | 0 credits, code-drawn (`anim_line.py` with a gold palette) + AI narrator |
| @whatif.overload (new) | **Pixel art**: retro game look, HUD bars, "LV 12", week counters as levels (sample `whatif-pixel-art.jpg`) | "what if you did X for N weeks" reads like a game; it fits release-week gaming culture (GTA 6) without using anyone's IP | 0 credits, code-drawn at 135×240 and scaled ×8 |

## Consequences
- After this week's tests, @earndapp stops making line-art videos; that style moves to the line-art account full-time. @earndapp's video slot returns to brand formats (data-chart, app POV, A vs B with app screens).
- Earnie's anatomy edu stays, redrawn as comic "lesson" panels over time.
- Never: the brick-toy style (a trademarked toy look), a game's own art or fonts, real people's photos.
