## The structure
- Dark stage, two white 3D mannequins side by side under spotlights, each with a coloured label (orange vs blue).
- Title in the top third, "**A vs B.**" with each word in its side's colour, plus an optional sub-question ("Which builds bigger legs?").
- A counter (weeks, reps in reserve, kg) ticks while both lift, then the result is revealed. Epic piano/cinematic music.
- Caption = the hook question + "are you the left or the right? 👇".

## Why it works
- A two-sided question everyone has an opinion on means comments, and you have to watch to the end to see the answer.
- The comparisons are real training debates (failure vs RIR, light vs heavy, full vs half ROM, progressive overload), so they get saved, not just watched.
- A simulation feels like "proof" without a face or a voice.
- Cinematic music sets it apart from the usual phonk gym edits.

## Our rules for it
- Outcomes are told as strength, reps and what the research says, never physique promises ("watch the glutes" is out). Weights in progressions are labelled "example" (except @whatif.overload joke escalations, founder 2026-11-04).
- The answer must be correct: cite the consensus (e.g. light vs heavy: similar growth when sets go close to failure; 0 vs 2 RIR: similar growth, less fatigue).
- The winner side lines up with EARND: same programme + tracked, add a rep when you can, 2 hard sets.
