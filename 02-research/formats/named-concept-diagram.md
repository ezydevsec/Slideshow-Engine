# Format: named-concept diagram (45–55 s, narrated, one evolving diagram)

Source (founder link 2026-11-11): @lockd..in (9.1K followers, 31 videos; bio sells a "make videos like this" course), posted 2026-11-07: **1.2M views, 108K likes, 15.2K saves, 9.8K shares**, 51 s, own voice. Frames: `02-research/swipe/2026-11-11-lockdin-rubber-band-frames.jpg`.

## Script (transcribed)
"Your friends like you the way you are. That's the problem." → they want you to win "as long as winning doesn't change anything" → skip one night out and you've changed; stop drinking and it's a joke; leave early and it's "who do you think you are?" → "Every group has a shape… when you move, the whole thing has to stretch. So it pulls. **That's the rubber band.**" → two people leave for the same dream: the first feels the pull and comes back, the second lets it snap and has a lonely year → "If nobody's pulling you back, you're not going anywhere."

## What's different from the other line-art formats
- **One diagram the whole way through** (a circle of friends with a band stretching toward a dream flag) that stretches, snaps and splits as the story moves. Small labels (NIGHT OUT, NO DRINKS, LEAVE EARLY) tick in as checkboxes.
- **The concept gets a name** and a stamp on screen ("RUBBER BAND"), so the idea is easy to remember and repeat. That's what drives shares.
- **Word-by-word captions at the top** in a clean sans with one word in italic serif ("shape").
- Paradox hook in 2 seconds ("…That's the problem").
