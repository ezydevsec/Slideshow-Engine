# Anime coach explainer: character concepts (draft)

Drafted 2026-10-28 for **@earndapp**, narrated video via Higgsfield. Status: **awaiting founder pick**. Nothing generated yet.
Format reference: `anime-coach-explainer.md`.

## Rules every concept follows

- Original character. No Baki likeness, name, scars, hair, or footage, and no cloned voice of a real actor.
- Art style: gritty high-contrast black-and-white manga, hard shadows, black background (style borrowed from both references, not a specific character).
- One accent colour: **EARND lime `#C6FF00`**, replacing the reference's purple. It ties every video to the brand palette.
- The narrator talks to the viewer ("you"). He never claims his own results or struggles. No "I used to have skinny arms".
- No physique promises. Payoffs land on tracking, targets and having a plan ("you can see when you've improved").
- Numbers on screen are labelled "example". The app moment is the real `01-app/assets/exercise-target.png` screenshot, never a generated phone.
- Prelaunch close: "EARND launches soon. Follow for the launch."
- @earndapp is the brand account, so the EARND tie is self-evident. The caption still names EARND, and the post is marked AI-generated on TikTok.

## Concept A: "The Coach"

- **Role:** a mentor who narrates and appears on screen, watching and correcting.
- **Look:** late 40s, broad, heavy brow, shaved head, short grey beard, old tracksuit jacket open over a thick frame. He carries a **beaten-up training notebook** with lime page tabs. The notebook is his prop and the bridge to the app.
- **Accent:** lime notebook tabs and a lime pencil.
- **Voice:** very deep, slow, short sentences with pauses. Shortlist: **Vlad** or **Gideon** (see Voice below).
- **Sample hook (Baki cadence):** "you trained hard last week. / what did you lift? / you don't know. / then how will you beat it?"
- **App bridge:** he flips the notebook shut, and the cut goes to the real EARND screen: "EARND shows your last session and today's target."
- **Why:** the notebook makes "tracking" a visual, not a feature list. It hits pain #1 ("can't remember what I lifted") and the magic moment directly.
- **Risk:** a mentor talking down to the viewer can feel preachy. Keep him terse, not lecturing.

## Concept B: "The Lifter" (closest to the reference format)

- **Role:** a silent demonstrator who stands in for the viewer. A separate deep narrator voices him in second person.
- **Look:** early 20s, lean but not skinny, messy dark hair, shirtless, **lime training shorts** (a direct swap for the reference's purple). He's the same character in every video, so it becomes a series.
- **Accent:** lime shorts, plus lime fills on the muscle diagrams.
- **Voice:** deep narrator (**Gideon** or **Sterling**), slightly less menacing than A.
- **Sample hook (explainer cadence):** "your arms look fine from the side. / then you turn around. / you've been skipping one muscle."
- **App bridge:** he checks his phone between sets, and the cut goes to the real EARND screenshot: "EARND shows what to beat on every set."
- **Why:** this copies the proven format's mechanics most closely (one recurring everyman, diagrams, demos). The viewer projects himself onto him.
- **Risk:** a lean-to-bigger arc across videos would read as a physique promise. He stays the same build in every video, with no before/after.

## Concept C: "The Shadow"

- **Role:** a faceless, hooded figure. Pure Baki-reference intensity, made for "what if" motivational hooks.
- **Look:** a hooded silhouette with the face in deep shadow, only the eyes catching light. Heavy chalked hands and a worn lifting belt.
- **Accent:** lime light on the eyes and the chalk.
- **Voice:** the deepest available (**Vlad**), very slow, near-whisper menace.
- **Sample hook:** "what if every set today / had to beat last week's? / would you even know the number?"
- **App bridge:** the hood turns toward the camera, and the cut goes to the real EARND screen.
- **Why:** it has the strongest stop-scroll mood and matches the Baki reference's energy most closely. No face means a consistent look is cheaper to keep across videos.
- **Risk:** heavy on mood and light on teaching. It suits short (15–35 s) motivational cuts more than 90 s explainers.

## Recommendation

**A, with B's diagram style.** The Coach narrates and appears on screen. Anatomy and exercise beats use the reference's line-art diagrams with lime fills. His notebook gives every video a natural bridge into the EARND screenshot, which is the message we're testing. Keep C as a second test for short motivational hooks if A gets traction.
