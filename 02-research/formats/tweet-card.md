# Format: tweet card (Cal AI ad style)

Added 2026-11-07 from a Cal AI paid Instagram ad run through @drbeckycampbell (founder screenshot): a tweet-style card (avatar, name, handle, one sentence "If you're trying to X, stop guessing Y. This app does Z") above a phone screenshot of the app.

## Why it works
- Reads like a native post, not an ad; one sentence = problem + promise.
- "Stop guessing ___" hook, then proof right underneath (the real screen).
- Borrowed credibility from a known person (theirs is a paid doctor endorsement).

## Our rules applied
- Only our own accounts as the "author": @earndapp, @ezyxbt (founder, says it's his app), @earnie.lifts (says EARND is ours). Never a fake person, fake doctor or invented endorsement; never another platform's logo or a verified badge we don't have.
- Real screens only (`01-app/assets/`), "example" tag when weights show. No weight-loss or physique claims: our promise is "know what to beat", "stop guessing", "move a session".
- Renderer: `.claude/skills/slideshow-engine/scripts/tweetcard_render.py` (tweets.json), 1080×1350.

## Where to use it
1. **Paid ads at launch** (best fit: it *is* an ad format): Meta/TikTok Spark ads from @earndapp and @ezyxbt.
2. **Organic, now:** as the cover (slide 1) of a 5-slide set, then the usual value/app/CTA slides. A single-image organic post would need a founder-approved exception (only @ezyxbt one-liners have one).
3. **@earnie.lifts:** tweet + meme image ("me: … / me next week:") is a native meme format on IG.
