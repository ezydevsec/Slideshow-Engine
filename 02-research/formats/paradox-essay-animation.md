# Format: narrated paradox essay, minimal glowing pictograms (45–70 s)

Source: @cryothum "School rewards knowing, Money rewards trying" (2026-11-07): **1.5M views, 231K likes, 33K saves, 34.6K shares** (2.3% share rate, very high), 68 s, own voiceover. Account: 62K followers from 17 videos; bio sells an animation course. Frames: `02-research/swipe/2026-11-09-cryothum-frames.jpg`.

## Script (transcribed)
0 s "The smartest person you know is probably broke, and the reason why explains almost everything about money." → the smartest kid at school (IQ 160, $0) → 20 years later, an average job → "school and money play by opposite rules": school punished guessing, money pays for acting without certainty → he keeps searching for the perfect plan while "someone far less gifted launches something embarrassing and figures it out along the way… until people start calling it luck."

## Structure
1. **Paradox hook (0–6 s):** a counter-intuitive claim about someone you know ("the smartest person you know is probably broke").
2. **One character, one story:** a pictogram figure with a label (graduation cap), with numbers on screen (IQ 160, $0, +20 yrs).
3. **The rule flip:** "X and Y play by opposite rules" with two icons and arrows.
4. **The rival:** a less gifted figure who acts, shown with a graph that climbs.
5. **The last line reframes it** ("people start calling it luck"). No CTA in the video.

Visual: black background, white glowing line-art pictograms, small pill labels, a monospace number, a dashed floor line, fog. Calm male narration, no music.

## Why it works
- The hook makes viewers think of a real person they know, so they share it ("this is literally my brother").
- One clear story, not a list of tips, so retention holds for 68 s.
- The minimal art keeps all attention on the voice, and it's cheap to make.

## Our gym version
- Same structure, our own art (EARND graphite + accent, our figures). Rendered in Python (shapes + glow) at **0 credits**, voice via ElevenLabs (Knox), a trending sound under it at 5–10% (standing rule).
- The winner is always the person who runs the same plan and knows the number to beat. Never physique promises: "he can see he's stronger than last month", never "he got huge".
- 45–60 s is allowed (voiced explainer exception to the 5–8 s video rule). The hook must land in the first 3 s.
