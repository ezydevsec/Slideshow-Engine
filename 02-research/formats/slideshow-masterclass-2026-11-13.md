# Notes: "TikTok Slideshows Masterclass" (founder paste 2026-11-13, by an agency owner) and what it changes for us

Summary in our words, then the gap against our system.

## The claims worth acting on
1. **Validate before you build.** A structure counts as proven when it has 50–100K+ views, is less than 30 days old, is easy to repeat at scale, and **several different accounts** are hitting with it (not one lucky post). Collect 5–10 examples per structure.
2. **Look outside your niche.** The best structures often come from unrelated niches (skincare, dating, self-improvement apps) and haven't been used in yours yet.
3. **Four skeletons cover almost everything:** pain funnel (slide 1 = a specific pain, product enters softly on slide 4); list/swap (5–7 items, always end on the product); identity (who the viewer wants to become: aesthetic images, short lines, steady not spiky); quick hit (2–3 slides, bottom of funnel).
4. **Slide 1 is the whole decision**: a strong image and a pain point in the text.
5. **Add text in TikTok's editor, not baked into the image.** Native text reads as organic.
6. **Volume plus a quality floor**, then **milk winners**: remake a hit with new images and wording until it stops clearing 100K, instead of chasing a new idea.
7. **Expect the spread**: most posts under 5K, a few per account at 50–500K. The more accounts and posts, the more reliable the hits.
8. **Judge on conversions, not views**, once there's something to buy.
9. **Comments matter**: a dead comment section loses buyers.

## Where we stand vs that
| Point | Us today | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Validation | Founder links go straight into the test queue | Add a **4-check gate** (50K+, <30 days, repeatable, 3+ accounts) before a format enters the queue; single-account outliers go to "watch" |
| Other niches | Founder's links already brought line-art (mindset niche); the daily scan only searches gym hashtags | Add **2 unrelated niches a day** to the scan (self-improvement apps, study/productivity apps, dating apps, skincare), slideshows only, last 30 days |
| Skeletons | Lots of list/swap (edu), a little pain funnel (`pain-funnel-test.md`), **no identity format**, no quick hit | Bring back the **pain funnel** on @earndapp; test **identity** on @ezyxbt ("the guy who…" aesthetic lines over his photos); quick hit after launch |
| Slide 1 | Covers are informational ("4 exercises. your whole back.") | Test **pain covers**: "3 months in and you still can't tell if you're stronger" |
| Native text | All text is baked into the image | **Test: clean images + text typed in TikTok** on a few posts a day (costs the founder ~1 min per post; the posting.md gives the exact lines) |
| Milking winners | winner_variation_share 0.6, but in practice we test a new format almost every slot | Our two best posts ever (**split ranking 1,864**, **"the wins just got smaller" 1,370**) have barely been remade. Remake each in new wording 5–10 times |
| Volume | 3 posts/day/account, 3 accounts (+2 on Saturday) | Fine for now; volume rises with accounts. Quality floor first |
| Conversions | Prelaunch, nothing to buy | From launch: **one App Store campaign link per account** so downloads can be tied to each account and format |
| Comments | Standing rule: our accounts never comment on each other | **Keep that rule.** Seeding fake "third-party" comments between our own accounts is inauthentic behaviour under TikTok's rules and misleading to buyers. Instead: reply to every real comment within the first hour, pin a brand comment (openly from the brand), end captions with a question people can answer |
