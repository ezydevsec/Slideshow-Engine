## Our version (@ezyxbt)
- Founder's own photos (dark/moody ones: b15-02, b16-01, b9-07, b9-08, b14-04…). Text in the upper third, never over the face.
- Lines are gym-life one-liners in his voice: dry, relatable, about consistency/tracking/the split. No physique promises, no profanity unless he says so.
- No app slide and no CTA on the image (that's what makes it work); the bio carries EARND. **Needs founder OK**: breaks the 5–10 slides / app-on-slide / end-CTA rules for this format only.
- Caption: one short first-person line + 3 hashtags (his caption rule), or none if he prefers the no-caption look.
- Sound: the week's top trending sound.
- Renderer: `native_render.py` slide type `oneliner` (`text`, `color`, `y`, `dim`).
