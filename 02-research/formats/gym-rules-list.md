## Slide by slide
1. **Hook.** "unspoken gym rules" title card. Lime on black, upper. The premise lifters already argue about.
2. **Rules 1–4.** One real etiquette rule per slide, white on black. At least one mildly debatable rule to pull comments (e.g. curling in the squat rack).
3. **Last rule = the turn.** A rule about knowing your numbers ("know what you lifted last time").
4. **The generic alternative.** What most people log with: a notes app full of numbers. Text only, no fake notes-app screenshot.
5. **App.** Real `exercise-target.png` screenshot, lime box with black text, direct EARND product language.
6. **CTA.** Callback to the last rule + the @earndapp prelaunch CTA.
