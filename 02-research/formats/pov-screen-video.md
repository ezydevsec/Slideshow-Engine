# Format: POV + screen video (@earndapp)

Added 2026-11-05 from a @calai.app ad (https://www.instagram.com/p/DcMM7SgswlF/, 11.5 s, no voiceover).

## Structure
1. **POV hook** (0–4 s): a real-life first-person shot of the problem moment with one bold line of text ("Please stop eyeballing your calories").
2. **Screen recording** (4–11 s): the app solving it in 2–3 quick steps (scan → result → logged). Music only.

## Our version
- Hook in our pain: "please stop guessing your weights", "stop writing your sets on paper", "missed monday? don't skip it".
- Screens: only real EARND screens. Until we have real screen recordings, use slow zooms on the real screenshots (`01-app/assets/demo-*.png`, inner phone screen), tagged "example" where weights show. Never AI-generated UI.
- POV shot: the founder's own 3–5 s gym clip (no other people) is best; AI POV is the fallback (AI label ON; crop out any fake app UI or logos the model draws).
- End on a 2 s card with the prelaunch CTA. 10–14 s total. Sound: a trending sound from that week's bank (no voiceover needed).
