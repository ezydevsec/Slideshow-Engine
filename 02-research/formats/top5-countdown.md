## Slide by slide (square 1080x1080, 8–9 slides)
1. **Hook** on the strongest portrait: "top 5 [muscle] exercises, ranked by [criterion]".
2. **Setup** (replaces the reference's "before" photo, which implied a transformation): the criteria and who the ranking is for.
3. **#5**, 4. **#4**, 5. **#3**, 6. **#2**: exercise name, then one line of reasoning against the stated criteria.
7. **Bonus tip = EARND**, second-to-last, where the references place their app. Founder relationship in the same slide ("i'm building EARND…"). Relaxed photo, not a physique shot.
8. **#1**: the pick and why, against the criteria.
9. **CTA**: "i'm building EARND. follow @earndapp for the launch."

## Where the app goes
Slide 7 (bonus tip), as the founder's own product. Never "I use EARND and I love it" or anything that reads as a neutral recommendation.

## Ranking rules
- State the criterion and the audience on slide 2 (e.g. "easiest to progress on, for beginner to intermediate lifters"). Rank against that, not "best ever".
- No invented scores ("8/10", "-1/10") and no scientific claims. One-line reasons a lifter can check for themselves.
- Never say one exercise or split is universally best.
- The rankings are the founder's opinion: **he confirms the picks before posting.**
- No before/after or transformation slides. Never imply EARND produced the founder's physique.
