## Step 2: taken apart (the gym version, 16 slides, 1.0M)
1. **Cover = plain small white text on black**: "Gym Progress Slideshow ideas (Part 18)". No hook design at all. It tells a specific viewer (someone about to post their own gym slideshow) "this is for you".
2. **Slides 2–16 are assets, not information**: moody black-and-white manga art (a silhouette holding an old ID card, a skinny vs big lifter, a sculptor carving himself with a quote "A dream without sacrifice is just a fantasy", a boxer "Never forget what you promised yourself that night", a long-haired swordsman "If you're not willing to suffer for your dreams…"), alternating with **blank "Picture 1 / Picture 2" slides** where the viewer drops in their own before/after.
3. **Why it works**
   - **Utility for creators → saves.** The viewer saves it to use it, not to remember it. Saves are the strongest ranking signal, so it travels (3.7–6.6% save rate vs ~1% for normal posts).
   - **"You may be looking for"** reads like a search result: it assumes the viewer has a need and answers it.
   - **Series ("Part 18")** → people follow for the next part.
   - **Swipe-through by design**: every slide is a candidate, so people look at all of them (fixes our "2.3 slides viewed" problem).
   - **Mood over words**: dark, high-contrast, one short quote at most; trending slowed audio ("STEEL TEKK – Slowed").
   - **No caption needed**, hashtags only.
4. **Risks for us**: their art is copyrighted manga (Baki, Berserk, Hajime no Ippo) → we use **our own Ryo art**. Before/after "Picture" slides invite physique transformations → our placeholders say "your week 1" / "your week 12" (a log, a lift), never a body.
