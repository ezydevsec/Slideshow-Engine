# Format: two-panel "dissolve" meme (single image, Instagram share-bait)

Source (founder screenshot 2026-11-12): @gamblersdaily (Instagram) "Are you free?" / "For gambling." **104K likes, 5.4K reposts, 208K shares** (twice as many shares as likes). Screenshot: `02-research/swipe/2026-11-12-gamblersdaily-are-you-free.png`.

## Why it works
- Two panels, one joke: a vague invite makes the person dissolve into dust; the one thing they love makes them fully solid again.
- **Built to be sent**: people DM it to the friend it describes, which is why shares beat likes 2:1. On Instagram, DM shares are the strongest reach signal.
- Zero reading effort: two short captions, one image.
