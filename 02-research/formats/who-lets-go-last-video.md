# Format: "who lets go last?" contest video (10–12 s, animated)

Source: @focus.habits (a focus app, 2.7K followers) "WHO LETS GO LAST?" posted 2026-11-01: **3.6M views, 177K likes, 13.7K saves, 3.7K shares**, 12 s, original sound. Frame saved in `02-research/swipe/2026-11-09-focus-habits-who-lets-go-last.jpg`.

## What it is
Dark stage, four identical figures dead-hanging from one bar under spotlights, each labelled (MOTIVATION · DISCIPLINE · OBSESSION · EGO), a monospace timer counting up. They drop one by one; one accent-coloured figure is left. Caption asks "which one are you holding on with? 👇", with the app's pitch in one line.

## Why it went viral (on a tiny account)
- **The question is the hook:** "who lets go last?" makes you pick an answer in the first second, so people stay to see if they were right. Retention ran to the end and the video loops.
- **A twist ending:** the one you'd expect (discipline) isn't the winner, which drives comments and shares (people argue in the comments).
- **No reading, no voice:** four words and a timer. It works in any language and without sound.
- **The app is only in the caption:** the video is pure entertainment, the pitch is one line under it.

## Our version
- Renders in Python (frames → ffmpeg) at **0 Higgsfield credits**. Our own look: EARND graphite + accent, gym setting, or Earnie figures on @earnie.lifts. Not a copy of their art.
- Winner is always the EARND idea, said as a mindset, never as a physique promise: "LAST WEEK'S NUMBER", "A PLAN", "THE LOG".
- Timer can count seconds (hang), reps (set), or weeks (who's still training in week 12).
- 10–12 s, the question at the top in the first frame, the winner lit in the accent colour, a 1 s hold on the winner, loop-friendly cut.
- Counts as the account's 1 video a day, and as the new-format test the first time it runs.
