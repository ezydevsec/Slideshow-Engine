# Pain funnel (initial creative test)
Skeleton: Pain funnel
Status: testing (**not a proven format**: no outlier research behind it yet)
Examples: none. Research mode was blocked (no Reddit or TikTok access). Replace with 3 real outlier links once research runs.

Used for the first three EARND sets. All three share this structure so the test compares **hooks**, not formats.

## Slide by slide
1. **Hook.** One pain, in the persona's voice. Stroke text, upper.
2. **Relatable.** What the pain looks like in the gym. Ends with a reason to swipe.
3. **Twist the knife.** The cost of the pain (guessing, scrolling notes, planning instead of lifting).
4. **App.** Real screenshot of the exercise-target screen, casual first-person overlay ("found an app that..."). Background text, upper.
5. **How it works.** The worked example: last session → today's target, labelled as an example.
6. **CTA.** Payoff line + "EARND launches soon. Follow for the launch."

## Where the app goes
Slide 4, introduced as "found an app that...". Prelaunch: never "i've been using".

## Notes
- Six slides, under 25 words each.
- No download CTA, trial reference, or physique promise.
- Hold scheduling content until a programme-summary or reschedule screenshot exists.
