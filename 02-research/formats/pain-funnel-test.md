# Pain funnel (initial creative test)
Skeleton: Pain funnel
Status: testing (**not a proven format**: no outlier research behind it yet)
Examples: none. Research mode was blocked (no Reddit or TikTok access). Replace with 3 real outlier links once research runs.

Used for the first three EARND sets: **three initial messaging-angle tests**. They share the slide structure, but slides 1–5 differ between sets, so results compare whole angles, not hooks in isolation.

## Slide by slide
1. **Hook.** One pain, in the persona's voice. Lime text on black, upper.
2. **Relatable.** What the pain looks like in the gym. Ends with a reason to swipe.
3. **Turn.** What should happen instead (last session beside today's sets, a clear list with targets).
4. **App.** Real screenshot of the exercise-target screen, with direct EARND product language ("EARND puts...", "EARND shows..."). Lime box, black text, upper.
5. **How it works.** A worked example (last session → today's target), always labelled "example" on the slide.
6. **CTA.** Payoff line + "EARND launches soon. Follow for the launch."

## Where the app goes
Slide 4, in direct EARND product language from the official brand account. Never invented user experiences, testimonials, or endorsements.

## Notes
- Six slides, under 25 words each.
- Look: solid black backgrounds, lime hook and CTA, white middle text. No photos needed.
- No download CTA, trial reference, or physique promise.
- Hold scheduling content until a programme-summary or reschedule screenshot exists.
