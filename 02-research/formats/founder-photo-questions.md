# Founder photo questions (initial creative test)
Skeleton: Quick hit
Status: testing (**not a proven format**)
Account: @ezyxbt only

## Slide by slide
1. **Hook.** A short question the viewer can't answer, over a real founder gym photo.
2. **Second question.** Same pattern, different photo.
3. **Turn.** One line on why it matters. No claims about the founder's own past experience unless he has confirmed them.
4. **CTA.** One line on what EARND does, then "i'm building EARND. follow @earndapp for the launch."

## Rules
- Square 1080×1080, real founder photos only, crop and resize only.
- Crop out other gym members' faces. Avoid prominent third-party logos.
- EARND appears on the final slide only. No slide-count labels.
- Don't pair EARND with physique shots in a way that implies the app produced the physique.
