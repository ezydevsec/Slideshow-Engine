## Slide by slide (1080x1920, 7–8 slides)
1. **Hook**: lime text on black. A question list, a mistakes list, or one practical rule.
2–6. **One point per slide.** Alternate lime background with black text and black background with white text, as the references alternate colours. Numbered.
7. **App slide**, second-to-last, where the references put the app: the real `exercise-target.png` screenshot, lime box with black text, direct EARND product language. Only when it supports the point.
8. **CTA**: payoff line + "EARND launches soon. Follow for the launch."

## Where the app goes
Second-to-last, as the official EARND account ("EARND shows…"). No first-person user claims, no download link while prelaunch.

## Content rules
- Mix three angles across sets: **questions** (to ask yourself), **common mistakes**, and **practical tracking advice**. Not only rankings.
- Any numbers on a slide are labelled "example".
- No physique promises; talk about tracking, targets and having a plan.
- Only claim what the screenshots show (previous sets, today's target, the rest timer starting after a set). Don't claim pre-filled weights or session rescheduling until a screenshot shows them.
