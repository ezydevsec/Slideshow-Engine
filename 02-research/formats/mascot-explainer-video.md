# Format: mascot explainer video (@earndapp)

Added 2026-11-05 at the founder's request ("i need videos like this").

## Reference
- @calai.app paid Instagram ad, 57 s, posted 2025-12-17: https://www.instagram.com/p/DSYA7PQjFgi/
- Read by an influencer (discount code at the end). A faceless graphite muscle mascot, similar to ours, carries the story; the middle section is real phone footage of the app.

## Structure (beats and rough timing)
1. **Simple rule** (0–6 s): mascot, plain blue-grey background. "Eat less, lose weight. Simple."
2. **"Well, in theory…"** (6–18 s): the mascot gets lost in maths: pencil-sketch equations and macro rings around him. Name the pain specifically.
3. **"That's exactly why…"** (18–20 s): brand card.
4. **The app doing it** (20–36 s): real app screens, one feature per line of voiceover.
5. **Gym moment + "if you're serious about…"** (36–42 s): mascot training, then pointing at the camera.
6. **End card** (last 2–3 s): headline, 3 benefit bullets, rating/downloads.

## Our version (rules applied)
- Brand voice (Knox), and "we built EARND". Never "I use it" or "from my experience": we can't invent user experiences.
- App section uses only real screenshots from `01-app/assets/` in a phone frame (the inner screen of the demo mockups, cropped). Never AI-generated phones or UI. Screens showing weights get the "example" tag.
- No physique or fat-loss claims. The pain is the mental maths: remembering last week, working out what to beat today.
- End card: 3 real features + the prelaunch CTA. No trial, rating or download counts (we have none) until launch.
- Captions bottom (y 1640) so they never cover the phone; phone cards sit higher (y 210).
- About 45 s. Cost: 4 mascot images + 4 Kling 5 s clips (about 40 credits) + voice and music (about 2 credits).

## Variations to test
- Same skeleton, different pain: "4 days a week. Simple. Well, in theory… you miss Tuesday and the whole week falls apart" → move session.
- "Add a rep. Simple. Well, in theory… was it 8 reps or 7?" → last session + target.
- A version for paid ads once launched (download CTA, real rating).
