## Why it went viral
1. **Paradox hook about an everyday thing** (0–6 s): "reacts to the lightest brush of your finger, and ignores a pen pressed as hard as you can." Everyone has seen it, nobody knows why → curiosity gap.
2. **Relatable moments**: gloves in winter, screen going crazy in the rain.
3. **Every sentence visualised**: premium dark 3D "magnified illustration", glowing gold lines, tiny tags (GLASS, WIRES, CHARGE), word-by-word captions with the key word highlighted.
4. **Calm, clear narration, one idea, no hard sell.**
5. **Reframe payoff**: "your screen never felt your finger… your finger was just the thing that took it."
6. **Share CTA aimed at a specific person**: "send this to someone whose phone never works with wet hands."
