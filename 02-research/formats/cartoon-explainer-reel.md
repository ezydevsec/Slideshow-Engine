# Format: cartoon explainer reel (@earndapp)

Added 2026-11-06 from @green.faction "This is how much time it takes to get your muscles back" (https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfNFqhdTR-x/): 2.4M plays and 149K likes in 4 days, 59 s, voiceover. Gym Faction is a fitness app whose mascot is a faceless green muscle guy.

## Why it works
- The app's own mascot in flat 2D cartoon illustrations on a solid brand-colour background; a new image every ~2–4 s with pop-in zooms (no real animation needed).
- Funny cutaways: zoom bubbles into muscle cells with cute little characters, the mascot in a lab coat with a clipboard, calendar props, big "3X"-style labels.
- Topic answers a common fear ("I took a break, is it all gone?"), cites research, reassures, ends on the app.

## Our version
- Graphite mascot, flat soft lime background (#CFEA6B), thick outlines, sticker style. Keep the mascot faceless and uniform graphite (recolour any lime the model paints on the chest).
- ~10 illustrations (≈ 20 credits), no Kling. Each still → pop-in zoom clip (ffmpeg zoompan), cuts on the voiceover beats, pop SFX on every cut, big Anton labels at the top (DAY 3, WEEK 1…), 2-word captions at the bottom.
- Science stated carefully (no physique promises; "seem to" where research isn't settled); payoff ties to a real app feature shown with its real screen; prelaunch CTA.
- 40–45 s. Voice Knox, playful pizzicato/marimba bed.

## Topic bank
- Missed a week: what actually happens (2026-11-06 → move session)
- Why you're sore 2 days later, not right after (DOMS; sore ≠ progress → track)
- How long a set should take you to recover from (2–3 min rest → rest timer)
- What actually happens in your first month at the gym (neural → plan + targets)
- Does it matter what time you train? (consistency > timing → move sessions, keep 4)
