## Structure
- Black void, glossy reflective floor, warm spotlight pools. White 3D-looking stick figures with round heads; the "right" one glows, the other greys and slumps.
- Two (or a crowd vs one) side by side doing **the same thing**; one variable differs (adds weight / tracks / starts now / lays the next brick).
- Mono HUD at the top: "WEEK 1 >>", a one-line premise ("same gym. same days."), a label over each figure with its number.
- 11–16 s, no voice, music only. The last 2 s land the line ("same effort. only one added weight.").
- No talking, no faces, no logos. Loops cleanly.
