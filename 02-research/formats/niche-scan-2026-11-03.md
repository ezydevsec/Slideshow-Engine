## Takeaways
1. The two biggest slideshow audiences in the niche are **women's gym tips** and **beginner splits**. A fitness app (@workitapp) is already winning with exactly our play: a split slideshow plus the app in the caption.
2. **Relatable meme slideshows** break out from accounts with under 3K followers, which makes them the best fit for a fresh account.
3. Recipe slideshows are proven (@mayaviefit) but bridge to EARND only weakly, since EARND is a training app.
