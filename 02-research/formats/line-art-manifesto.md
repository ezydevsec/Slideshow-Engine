# Format: line-art manifesto (40–50 s, narrated, one icon per line)

Source (founder link 2026-11-11): @credora07 (13.1K followers, 31 videos), posted 2026-11-08: **1.0M views, 222K likes, 55.6K saves (5.6%), 18.8K shares**, 46 s. Sound: **Les (Instrumental Slowed), aurelia & dopuu** (the same track @appforthegym used). Frames: `02-research/swipe/2026-11-11-credora07-manifesto-frames.jpg`.

## What it is
Same visual family as @nolledge and @cryothum (white line art on pure black, tiny caps labels), but **no story**: a rapid-fire spoken manifesto, about 13 short lines, **one minimal icon per line**: eyes circling a figure ("JUDGEMENT"), arrows pointing at a figure ("THEIR RULES"), a Y-shaped fork ("YOUR DECISION"), an X ("MISTAKES"), an hourglass, a "6 MONTHS" dial, a checklist ("NO EXCUSES"), a figure walking toward a sunrise ("LIVE YOUR LIFE").

The original swears a lot ("f*** anyone who judges you…"). We keep the rhythm and the icons and drop the swearing.

## Why it works
- **Second person, every line**: each line feels aimed at the viewer, so they save it to rewatch and send it to a friend (5.6% saves, 1.9% shares).
- **A new image every 2–3 seconds**: the icon changes on every line, so there's always something new to look at.
- **Slowed instrumental under a calm, intense voice**: the same audio mood as the other two line-art formats.

## Our version
- Rendered with `anim_formats.py` (glow line art) at 0 credits plus one voiceover.
- Gym manifesto in our voice. The lines point to the method: one plan, the same exercises, write it down, beat it by one rep, show up on bad weeks. No physique promises, no swearing.
- @earndapp: video slot. @ezyxbt: photo version ("the rules i train by", one rule per photo). @earnie.lifts: must be a story, so the gym-anxiety angle becomes "Earnie's first day: everyone was watching. Nobody was."

Glowing white figures with a light-bulb head, one scene per line (people laughing, a "JUN" calendar loop of the same year repeating, a mountain that's "TOO BIG", a clock for "PERFECT MOMENT?", a checklist for "OBSESSED", a target for "RUTHLESS FOCUS").

## The pattern across the founder's links
5 of the last 6 links (@cryothum, @nolledge, @credora07, @lockd..in, @ascendxlabs) are the **same family**: white line art on black, one calm intense voice, 45–60 s, 1–5% saves. The two biggest manifestos (@credora07 1.0M, @ascendxlabs 831K) share **one topic: other people's judgement** ("stop caring what people think"). The gym version of that topic is gym anxiety ("everyone's watching me"), which is one of the biggest fears for beginners. So our first manifesto is: **"Nobody at the gym is watching you."**
