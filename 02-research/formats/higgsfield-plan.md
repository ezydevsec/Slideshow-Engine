# Higgsfield usage plan

Agreed with the founder 2026-10-28. Higgsfield is the production studio for @earndapp visuals. Evidence: `anime-coach-explainer.md` (TikTok data).

## Use it for

1. **Original character series on @earndapp** (anime coach explainer). Character stills → animated clips → narrator voice (preset **Vlad**). Run it as a series: the same character, Part 1/2/3, one pain point per episode, with the real EARND screenshot as the payoff. Keep episodes short (about 15–35 s).
2. **Slideshow visuals for @earndapp:** lime-on-black muscle diagrams, gym scenes, character panels. These are backgrounds only, never app UI.
3. **Narrated versions of winning slideshows:** the same slides plus a Vlad voiceover, to test whether video lifts a proven message.
4. **Virality predictor:** pick between hook variants before the founder approves one.

## Never use it for

- AI influencers or AI "UGC creators" reviewing or endorsing EARND (invented testimonials).
- Generated app UI, phone mockups, or altered screenshots. App zooms are done locally from `01-app/assets/`.
- Anything with the founder's face or body (generation, motion transfer, AI upscaling). @ezyxbt stays real footage and photos, crop and resize only.
- Baki or any existing character's likeness, or a cloned real voice.
- Publishing. Higgsfield's TikTok publish would bypass the approval gate, so everything still goes through chat approval and Humanpost.

## Rollout

1. Start the free 3-day Plus trial (100 credits, MCP only, auto-renews at $49/mo unless cancelled).
2. Redo the young fighter character on a better image model; founder approves the look.
3. Episode 1 ("what did you lift?", `04-sets/2026-10-28-coach-what-did-you-lift/`), animated: about 50 credits.
4. Post on @earndapp next to the existing slideshows. Log views, profile visits and follows in `07-results/performance.csv`.
5. If it beats the slideshows on views per post, make episodes 2–3 on other pain points and move to Plus monthly. If not, stop.
