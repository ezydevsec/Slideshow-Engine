**Saves are the engine.** These are reference posts people keep for the gym, and saves push distribution. Arrow's 2026 posts copy bluebro's formats almost one to one (the same "ranking splits" FBEOD slide, the same "choose one of each exercise" push/pull days). The format works across accounts.

## Content types that win (both accounts)
1. **"The perfect push / pull / leg / upper day":** one exercise per muscle, sets × reps, one form cue per slide ("choose one of each and you've built the perfect push day")
2. **Rankings and tier lists:** "ranking every gym split worst → best", "biceps tier list F → S", "ranking every chest exercise"
3. **"Only 3 exercises you need for X":** shoulders (front/side/rear), abs, arms
4. **Full programmes:** "6-day program", "4-day upper/lower split", with every day and exercise listed (caption repeats the plan in text)
5. **Mindset:** "the power of consistency" (Arrow, 1.3M, Aug 2026)

## Visual system
- **3:4 portrait** (960×1280 / 1080×1350), not 9:16
- Light neutral background (cream/off-white; bluebro sometimes pastel yellow)
- Bold condensed headline (all caps on covers), body in a clean sans
- **One accent colour.** Arrow's is green (#3CD63C-ish), very close to our lime. Used on the muscle highlight and key words.
- **Illustration on every slide:**
  - bluebro: a recurring faceless **blue mascot** doing each exercise, plus anatomy figures with worked muscles in red
  - Arrow: faceless grayscale anatomy figure with the **target muscle filled green**; grid slides of 6 exercises with sets × reps
- Numbered items ("3. Close-Grip Cable Row / 2 sets x 6-8 reps / • one cue"); star ratings on rankings; weekday grid showing training days
- Small brand mark (Arrow logo top-left; bluebro handle top-right). **No app screenshots, no hard sell.** The CTA is the bio link.

## Fit with EARND
- **Almost perfect.** EARND *is* a 4-day Upper/Lower programme with targets to beat. "Ranking every split", "the perfect upper day", "4-day upper/lower" are our product's home turf.
- Our engine already makes photo slideshows; it needs a light-background **edu template** (or a dark version on brand), a 3:4 canvas, and numbered exercise cards.
- **Mascot:** the approved Baki-style fighter (`04-sets/2026-10-28-explainer-ep1-arms-width/frames/f00-character-reference.jpg`) can do what bluebro's blue mascot does, performing each exercise, so our posts look like ours.

## Rules to apply
- No physique promises in hooks or titles ("build the perfect upper day" is fine; "grow huge arms" is not).
- Don't claim what's inside EARND's programme (exercise list, sets) until the founder confirms it. Templates are general advice; the last slide bridges to EARND tracking with the real screenshot plus the prelaunch CTA.
- Sets × reps on templates are recommendations, not progression examples. Founder to confirm whether the "example" label rule applies to them.
- Generated illustrations mean `ai_images: true` and the TikTok AI label on.
