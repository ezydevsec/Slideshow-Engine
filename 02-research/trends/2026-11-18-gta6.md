Caveats for the catch: untrained men, one exercise, and the break group came straight back to training.

**Where the "you lose muscle within a week" line comes from:** Chris Beardsley (Patreon, Feb 2025, "Atrophy occurs within a training week"): detraining studies show measurable loss of muscle thickness within ~1 week, immobilisation within 48 h. That's a different creator, and it's about measurable thickness changes, not "your gains are gone".

**What the studies say (verified):**
- Ogasawara 2013: beginners who took **3 weeks off twice** ended 24 weeks with about the same muscle and strength as those who never stopped.
- Bosquet 2013 meta-analysis (103 studies): stopping training reduces strength, and the loss grows with how long you stop and depends on training status and age.
- Review of trained athletes: strength holds for **up to ~3 weeks**, then drops faster (5–16 weeks).
- Early "flat" look in week 1 is largely glycogen and water, which come back within days of training (secondary sources; not on a slide as a number).

**Honest one-liner for the GTA week:** "a week off won't erase your gains. a few weeks off costs a little, and it comes back fast. what costs you is not coming back."
