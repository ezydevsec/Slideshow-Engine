## Verified study for the window
- **Ogasawara et al. 2013** (Eur J Appl Physiol, online 2012; paper on Springer, DOI 10.1007/s00421-012-2511-9): 14 untrained young men, bench press 3×/week for 24 weeks. One group trained straight through; the other took **3 weeks off, then 6 weeks on, twice**. After 24 weeks, **muscle growth and strength were about the same**. Caveats for the catch: untrained men, one exercise, and the break group came straight back to training.
