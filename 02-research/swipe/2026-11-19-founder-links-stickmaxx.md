Gate: the 3D stickman comparison already passed on 11-15 (@stickmaxx, @neuronvisuals, @focus.habits). These are 27–28 days old, so still inside the 30-day window.

## Shot-by-shot structure (same in all three)
- **Look:** two white 3D stick figures (ball head, capsule limbs), black void, each on its own small lifting platform with warm glowing floor tiles, coloured bumper plates. The progressing figure **glows brighter** step by step; the other turns grey.
- **Layout:** side by side (deadlift, squat) or **stacked top/bottom panels** (bench).
- **Top text:** big pixel-mono counter "WEEK 1 / MONTH 1" with ">>" fast-forward marks, small subtitle "same gym. same days." / "same bench. same effort."
- **Labels per side:** left "SAME WEIGHT · squat: 60 kg"; right "+10 KG A MONTH · squat: 70 → 120 kg"; a popup at every jump: "+0 KG" (grey) vs "+10 KG" (gold).
- **Rhythm:** one rep per jump, ~1 s per month/fortnight, a few camera push-ins and close-ups on the plates being added; 13–16 s.
- **Ending:** "SAME EFFORT. / ONLY ONE ADDED WEIGHT." (or "same squats. different legs.") over a **physique reveal** (one figure muscular, flexing in a mirror), then loops to month 1.

## EARND version (what stays ours)
- Our own figures, made with Higgsfield 3D (start frames + Kling 3.0 pro), our wording, our text baked on with `hf_compose.py`.
- **Ending changed (no physique promises):** instead of the muscular reveal, the progressing figure's number climbs on screen ("BENCH: 40 → 70 kg, example"), then the **real EARND app screen** showing the next target, and the end card. Caption ends "which one are you? 👇".
- Weights are progression examples, so they carry a small "example" tag (CLAUDE.md numbers rule).
- Realistic jumps: +2.5 kg a week / +5 kg a month, not +5 kg a week on everything.
