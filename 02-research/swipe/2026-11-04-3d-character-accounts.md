# 3D recurring-character accounts (outlier research, 2026-11-04)

Source: Apify Clockworks TikTok Scraper, latest 15 videos per profile, pulled 2026-11-04.
Both are **videos (50 to 110 s), not photo-mode slideshows.**

## @suppose.there.was.time ("The original Bob")
- Account created about 2026-10-18. 16 videos, 118.4k followers, 2.3M likes.
- Every video in the sample got 240k to 4.0M views. Floor about 240k, median about 490k.
- Top: "When it can't wait while you're driving" 4.0M; "Bed time conversations" 2.7M; "When you make the wrong choice" 2.1M.
- Format: one 3D-animated recurring character (Bob) in everyday couple situations. Situational humour, no dialogue-heavy script.
- Captions: "When ...", "Send this to ...", "Tag the person who ...". Shares are high (6k to 174k), so the share prompt in the caption seems to work.
- One post a day at about 20:00 UTC. Same trending sound on every video ("Sad and lonely"). Tags: #couplehumor #animatedstory #relationships, plus #zackdfilms (borrowing an established 3D-explainer niche).
- Bio monetisation: Linktree.

## @whatif.physics3d (Nigel; 50M views claimed on its Instagram)
- 28 videos, 7.2k followers. Views are very uneven.
- Hits: "What if every 'huzz' was 100x louder than the last?" 2.2M; "What if Nigel said 'Miguel' faster and faster?" 1.9M; HUZZ Part 3 85k.
- Misses: interactive zombie series "THE MARCH" 412 to 10k views per day. Choose-your-path series did not travel.
- Format that works: **"What if X kept escalating?"** One rule applied repeatedly (each step 100x louder, speed doubles) and pushed to absurd real-physics extremes (Mach 1, orbit, escape velocity). Real numbers in the caption, with "Nigel is a simulation" disclosed.
- Comment hooks: "Comment HUZZ", "Top comment picks Part 3". Parts built on a meme word ("huzz").

## What travels (both accounts)
1. A recurring, named 3D character the viewer recognises.
2. One simple premise per video, stated in the first second.
3. Escalation or a relatable payoff, no explanation needed.
4. Share or comment prompt in the caption.
5. Consistent posting time and sound.

## What does not
- Long serial stories that need earlier parts (THE MARCH).

## Bridge to EARND (proposal, not yet a proven format)
- **What-if escalation applied to tracking or progressive overload.** For example: "What if he added 2.5 kg to the bar every week and never missed one?" The weekly steps escalate to absurd real-world weights (a fridge, a car, a jet), with real masses and every number labelled "example". The payoff is a joke, never a physique promise. CTA is the theme page's prelaunch line.
- **Relatable gym-situation humour with a recurring lifter.** For example: "When you forget what you lifted last week", "Send this to the friend who 'just eyeballs it'". It maps straight onto the pain bank.
- The character must be original, not a copy of Bob, Nigel or the Zack D. Films style.
- Any app appearance uses a real screenshot from `01-app/assets/` only.
- This would run on a new **theme** account, which needs the user to change the "no theme pages yet" rule. The account must say EARND is ours.
